I built Fold with Claude Code for leaders who are not data analysts. This page describes the first version, the one in the public demo and repository.

## The problem

Student organization leaders collect attendance however it arrives: a messy paste from a sign-in sheet, Instagram handles on a napkin, names half remembered after the fact. It ends up in spreadsheets and group chats, and in Notion docs nobody updates after week three. When a leader asks "who came to the last three events but went quiet this week?", nobody has the answer in one place.

## What it does

### Intake

A leader pastes whatever they collected: names, phone numbers, Instagram handles, in whatever shape a co-leader wrote them down. Claude turns that into structured student records and checks for duplicates in the same pass, so a typo doesn't create a phantom freshman.

### Events and attendance

A leader creates an event and marks who came with a quick-add form. The event page then shows who was there for the first time and who invited whom.

### Students

Each person on the roster carries their own history: how often they come, who brought them, who they've brought, and every attempt to reach them. A Gone Cold tab lists anyone who hasn't shown up in 30 days or more.

### Funnel

People move through stages from new to engaged. A sweep moves them between stages based on attendance, and filters show stale responses, missing follow-ups, and people stuck in one stage too long.

### Rides

Carpools are their own records. A leader assigns drivers and riders within seat limits, with separate sessions for the trip there, the trip back, and Sunday morning.

### Questions in plain English

A leader asks for "all freshmen who were invited by someone" and gets a table back. The model turns the question into a query that is validated against the schema before it runs.

## Under the hood

Fold is a Next.js 15 app on the App Router, in TypeScript end to end, with data in Turso, a hosted SQLite, and Drizzle ORM for the schema and migrations. Claude handles parsing and insights, called from server actions so the prompts and parsing logic stay on the server.
